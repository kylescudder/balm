Balm

A native, keyboard-driven Jira client for Mac and iPhone. The calm antidote to the browser tab you keep losing.

$ brew tap kylescudder/tap
$ brew install --cask balm

Fully native

SwiftUI on macOS and iOS. No web views, no Electron — list, board, and issue detail render like the system apps they sit beside.

Your data stays yours

Balm talks directly to Atlassian's API from your device. No analytics, no tracking, no servers reading your issues.

Notifications, quietly

An inbox for the issues you watch, report, or own — with read state that follows you between your Mac and iPhone.
