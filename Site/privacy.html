Balm

Privacy policy

Effective 17 July 2026

The short version: your Jira data moves directly between your device and Atlassian. Balm runs no servers that see, store, or analyse it, and collects no analytics of any kind.

What Balm is

Balm is a native macOS and iOS client for Jira Cloud, developed by Kyle Scudder. You sign in with your own Atlassian account using OAuth 2.0; Balm then reads and writes your Jira data on your behalf, from your device.

Data on your device

  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the system Keychain on your device, and nowhere else.
  • App data — your preferences, saved filters, and the notification inbox (issue keys, summaries, and who did what) — is cached in the app's local storage so the app works quickly and offline.
  • System notifications, if you enable them, are generated on your device. Nothing is pushed to it from a server.

Data in your Atlassian account

To keep read/unread state consistent between your devices, Balm saves a small record (the ids of notifications you've read) as a hidden property on your own Atlassian user account. It is visible to no other user, and revoking Balm's access severs the app from it.

The sign-in service

One small service, auth.balm.kylescudder.co.uk, exists because Atlassian requires the app's client secret when exchanging sign-in codes for tokens, and a secret cannot ship inside an app. During sign-in and token refresh, this service relays your authorization code or refresh token to Atlassian and returns the result to your device. It processes these values in transit only: it has no database, stores nothing, and logs no token contents.

What Balm never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data to sell.
  • No third-party services beyond Atlassian itself.

Your controls

  • Sign out in Balm's settings to remove tokens and cached data from the device.
  • Revoke Balm's access entirely at id.atlassian.com → Connected apps.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored locally.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. The policy will never change to permit collection this version rules out without a clear notice.
